1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy governs your use of the mobile game application Arrow Target Clash (Package Name: com.blueapps.arrowtargetclash), developed and published by BlueAppBox ("we", "us", or "our").

We respect your privacy and are committed to maintaining a transparent, privacy-first gaming experience. This policy complies with the Google Play Developer Program Policies, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

Arrow Target Clash does NOT collect, harvest, store, share, or sell any Personally Identifiable Information (PII). When you download, install, and play this game:

  • We do not require user accounts, email sign-ups, or social logins.
  • We do not collect your real name, physical address, email address, or telephone number.
  • We do not access your device contacts, address book, photo gallery, camera, or microphone.
  • We do not track or access your precise GPS location or real-time physical coordinates.
  • We do not collect payment card numbers, banking credentials, or biometric identifiers.

3. Local Device Storage (Offline Gameplay Data)

All gameplay progress and user configurations are stored exclusively on your local device (using standard browser HTML5 localStorage / private application sandbox storage):

  • Campaign Progression: Completed target stage index (Levels 1 to 300), highest unlocked level, and star rating records (1–3 stars per level).
  • Virtual Game Balances: In-game earned stars (⭐) and remaining hint tokens (💡) used to solve target gridlocks.
  • Player Preferences: Audio sound effect toggles (arrow release and clash SFX), active visual arena theme (Nebula Clash, Deep Galaxy, Supernova), and unlocked quiver liveries in the Arrow Quiver Arsenal.

Notice: This data never leaves your client device and is never uploaded to any external server or cloud database under our control.

4. Hardware APIs & Device Permissions

Arrow Target Clash utilizes only non-sensitive, standard device APIs strictly for core in-game mechanics:

  • Web Audio API: Synthesizes real-time archery audio frequencies (bow string releases, arrow flight whooshes, tip-to-tip clash recoils, and victory fanfare) mathematically inside device memory. No microphone input is ever monitored or requested.
  • Vibration API (Haptics): Generates subtle tactile feedback during arrow clash recoils and target clears. This can be muted at any time through your device system vibration settings.
  • Android System Navigation Bridge: Coordinates standard Android hardware back-button navigation and rewarded ad display callbacks without harvesting hardware serial numbers, IMEI, or MAC addresses.

5. Third-Party Services & Rewarded Advertisements

Arrow Target Clash is free to play and supported by optional rewarded video advertisements (for example, to re-align arrow flight paths when arrows are blocked or to recharge depleted hearts). These ads are delivered by certified third-party advertising networks (such as Google AdMob or authorized ad partners).

These advertising networks may process pseudonymous, non-personally identifiable technical telemetry strictly for ad delivery, fraud prevention, and performance metrics:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID / GAID)
  • Device model, manufacturer, screen resolution, and operating system build
  • General coarse geographic region (country or city level derived from IP address)
  • Ad impressions, completion rates, and fraud prevention signals
How to Opt Out of Personalized Ads:
You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID at any time via your Android device settings:
Settings → Google → Ads → Reset Advertising ID / Delete Advertising ID.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Arrow Target Clash is a family-friendly casual arrow target puzzle game suitable for general audiences of all ages. We strictly adhere to COPPA, GDPR-K, and Google Play's Families Policy:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).
  • When child-directed settings or Families Policy rules apply, only contextual, non-personalized, family-safe advertisements certified under Google Play guidelines are served.
  • Parents or guardians with questions or concerns may contact us directly at blueappsmanager@gmail.com for immediate assistance.

7. Data Retention & Your Deletion Rights

Because all gameplay data is stored solely on your device, you have complete control over its retention:

  1. In-App Reset: Open Settings inside Arrow Target Clash and tap "Reset All Progress to Level 1" to instantly purge all records, stars, and customizations.
  2. Device Clear Data: In Android, navigate to: Settings → Apps → Arrow Target Clash → Storage → Clear Data & Cache.
  3. App Uninstall: Removing the app from your device automatically and permanently erases all associated local files.
